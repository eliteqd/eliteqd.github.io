About Us

运维更智能 部署更方便 应用更便捷

Established in 2001, our company covers an area of 17,000 square meters, with a plant area of 8,000 square meters. Our company has a professional cementation/good-year welt/injected producing line which can produce professional safety shoes with cementation construction, side stitching construction, good-year welt construction and injected construction. Our factory has set up physical labs with compression tester for steel toe and steel plate, impact tester for steel toe, anti-static electrical instrument, dielectric resistant tester, EN whole sole flexing tester, DIN abrasion resistance tester, shoe head peeling tester, tensile strength tester, and so on.

Our qualified safety shoes with features of steel toe, steel plate, anti-static, oil resistant, and dielectric resistance have been European standard certified, and passed EN/ISO20345 and CE certifications. These help us become a major exporter in Europe, America and other countries and areas. With a view to explore domestic market, we have registered a new brand trademark ‘HI-UP’, and successfully passed ISO9002 assessment, together with labor safety production license.

Our motto is “satisfying all kinds of clients with superior quality”.
